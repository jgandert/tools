Help & keyboard shortcuts

  1. Choose a preset.

    Use the sidebar select or open the searchable library. The Topology group retains the original radius, tension, tangent, waveform, layout and transform controls.

  2. Edit and compare.

    Shape, Motion and Style share one preview and history. Stroke width is always at the top of the sidebar. Outlined particles have an independent outline width. Pin any design for matched-phase or real-time comparison; the inverted view checks the opposite colors.

  3. Preview and export.

    Check context previews, actual sizes, nearby variations, or your saved collection. Export animated SVG, a still SVG, a PNG, or an accessible HTML embed. Backgrounds and inverted colors are preview-only.
