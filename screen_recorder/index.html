Recording settings Mixed into the recording alongside any audio the browser shares. How often data is flushed while recording. Smaller values lose less on a crash, 1000 is a good default.

Good to know

  • Tab and system audio are only offered by some browsers and only for some sources — the status line reports what actually made it into the recording.
  • Screen capture needs a secure context: https:// or localhost.
  • Ending the share from the browser's own bar finishes the recording properly.
  • Nothing is uploaded and nothing is stored between visits — only these two settings are remembered.
