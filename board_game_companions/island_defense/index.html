Set up the table

One Spirit per player, one shared island. This guide covers the base game on balanced boards, without an Adversary, Scenario, Events, or expansion rules. Choose the player count and Blight option in Guide options.

Step 1 of 7 2 players · No Blight Card

Build one shared island

You win together by driving out the Invaders. Fear makes the victory condition easier; Dahan are the island's inhabitants, not extra Spirit pieces.

  • Take 2 balanced Island Board(s), one per player. Use the side with clearly separated, repeated terrain types—not the thematic map.
  • Join them using the standard layout for your player count in the physical rulebook. In solo play, use one board.
  • Place the Invader Board nearby. Keep its Fear area, Blight area, and Ravage / Build / Explore spaces visible.
  • Leave Adversaries, Scenarios, Events, and expansion tokens in the box for this guide.

First victory condition: at Terror Level I, no Explorers, Towns, or Cities may remain anywhere on the island.

Place starting pieces and supplies

  • On every Island Board, place the Explorers, Towns, Cities, Dahan, and Blight shown by its printed setup symbols.
  • Take this starting Blight from the box, not from the Blight supply you will prepare. Setup Blight does not destroy Presence or cascade.
  • Group the remaining Invaders, Dahan, and Energy as shared supplies. Keep unused Blight separate from the active Blight supply.
  • An Explorer, a Town, and a City are each one Invader. Buildings means Towns and Cities only.

Do not improvise extra setup pieces. Read each land's symbols, including those on boards not next to your seat.

Prepare Fear and Terror Levels

  • Put 8 Fear markers into the Fear Pool: 4 per player. Leave the Generated Fear area empty.
  • Shuffle the Fear Cards and take 9 without looking at their faces. Return the unused cards to the box.
  • Make three face-down groups of 3. From top to bottom: 3 cards, Terror Level II divider, 3 cards, Terror Level III divider, 3 cards.
  • Start at Terror Level I. Leave space for earned Fear Cards beside the deck; these stay face down until their effects resolve.

Every 8 Fear earns a card. Reset the same markers to the pool and continue counting any excess Fear.

Prepare the active Blight supply

For an introductory game, do not use a Blight Card. Put 11 Blight on the Invader Board's Blight space: 5 per player, plus 1 extra in total.

When this supply is exhausted, the loss condition is met. Finish the current action and anything it triggers before checking the result.

For play with a Blight Card, choose one at random without looking at its reverse. Place it Healthy Island side up and put 5 Blight on it: 2 per player, plus 1 extra in total.

When the Healthy Island side runs out, flip it immediately, add only the amount specified on its back, and follow its instructions. Flipping is not itself an automatic loss.

Official setup correction: the extra +1 applies once to the initial supply, in both modes. It is not per player, not an extra on the island, and is not added again when a card flips.

Build the Invader Deck

  • Sort the Invader Cards into Stages I, II, and III. Shuffle each stage separately.
  • Remove 1 random card from each stage, unseen. Return those 3 cards to the box.
  • Stack the remaining cards face down: 3 Stage I on top, then 4 Stage II, then 5 Stage III. Never shuffle the stages together.
  • Place the deck by Explore. Leave Ravage and Build empty for now. Do not reveal a card until the last setup step.

The Stage II flag is an Escalation reminder for an Adversary. With no Adversary, ignore the flag; still resolve the card's terrain normally.

Prepare each Spirit and the Power decks

  • Each player takes one Spirit Panel, its 4 Unique Power Cards, 13 Presence disks in one color, and the 3 matching Single-Turn Effect Markers.
  • For a first play, choose a low-complexity Spirit using the physical panels. Read only your chosen Spirit's setup and Special Rules; this guide does not teach individual Spirits.
  • Each Spirit follows its printed setup on a different starting board. Put the remaining Presence on the dashed track spaces, leaving the initial printed values uncovered. Start with all 4 Unique Power Cards in hand.
  • If using a printed Power Progression, set aside that Spirit's listed cards now. Otherwise you will gain cards using the normal draw-4 / keep-1 rule.
  • Shuffle the remaining Minor and Major Powers into separate face-down decks, each with its own discard pile. Leave room for each player's hand, played cards, and personal discard.

Energy is not a fixed setup grant. Follow your Spirit's setup. Normal Energy income happens during the first Spirit Phase, after Growth.

Perform the initial Explore

  • Reveal the top Invader Card. In each land of its terrain, check for an exploration source.
  • Add 1 Explorer if the land is Coastal, contains a Town or City, or is adjacent to a Town or City. An Explorer is not an exploration source.
  • Add at most 1 Explorer per matching land, however many sources qualify. Existing Invaders do not block this addition.
  • Slide the revealed card onto Build. Do not Build or Ravage during setup. Your first turn starts with the Spirit Phase.

First turn: Ravage is empty, so skip that step. The initial Explore card tells you where Invaders will Build.

Table is ready

2 Spirit(s) share a Fear Pool of 8 and start with 11 Blight in the active supply.

Check the table once: Ravage empty, initial Explore card on Build, Unique Powers in hand, and starting island Blight separate from the active supply.

Learn the first turn

Learn a turn

Everyone works through the same five phases. There are no clockwise player turns. Agree on an order when effects interact, and finish one Power or action before starting another.

Grow, gain Energy, play and pay

  1. Growth: choose the number of Growth options shown on your Spirit Panel—usually one group. Perform all of that group's effects if possible, in an order you choose. Pay any explicit cost.
  2. Add Presence: take the leftmost covered Presence from either track and place it within the indicated Range of your existing Presence. Follow any terrain restrictions. If adding twice, the first placement can extend the second placement's reach.
  3. Gain Energy: take the highest uncovered Energy value on your track. Do not add all uncovered numbers together. Energy gained during Growth is additional.
  4. Play and pay: choose cards from your hand up to your uncovered Card Plays limit. Play both Fast and Slow cards now and pay all their Energy costs now. You may play fewer cards or none.

Playing is not resolving. Played cards provide their Elements immediately; their text waits for the matching Power Phase. Energy gained from a Fast Power arrives too late to pay for cards in this Spirit Phase.

Reading your panel—without a Spirit tutorial

Presence tracks are not added together. Use the highest visible number on each track; visible Elements are additional. Special Rules apply when their text says. Innate Powers use Element thresholds and are explained below.

An instruction to add Presence may instead reposition one of your Presence already on the island, using the same placement restrictions. It does not recover destroyed Presence or uncover a track space.

Resolve Fast Powers before the Invaders

Use played Fast Power Cards and eligible Fast Innate Powers in an agreed order. Choose each target when you use the Power, not when you originally played the card.

  1. Speed

    Fast now; Slow after the Invader Phase.

  2. Range

    Measure from one of your Presence, or from your Sacred Site if required.

  3. Target

    Meet the land or Spirit restrictions at the moment of use.

  4. Effect

    Resolve top to bottom, in one target unless stated otherwise.

Range 0 means a land with your Presence. A Sacred Site means at least 2 Presence of the same Spirit in one land; one disk from each of two Spirits is not enough.

Elements and Innate Powers

Count Elements on your own played cards—including unresolved Slow cards—plus Elements from your tracks and other effects. Elements are requirements, not currency: using a threshold does not spend them.

Each Innate Power is normally usable once per turn, costs no Energy or Card Play unless it says otherwise, and must obey its Speed, Range, and target restrictions. Resolve every threshold you meet, from top to bottom; an "Instead" instruction replaces the specified earlier effect.

Check thresholds anew each turn; they are not permanent upgrades. You may choose to count fewer of your Elements when resolving a Power. Do not pool Elements between Spirits.

May I skip a Power or save it for later?

You may forgo a Power's entire text and still keep the card's Elements while it remains in play. There is no Energy refund. Once you use a Power, carry out its instructions as far as possible; impossible parts are skipped, not the rest.

An unused Fast Power cannot wait until the Slow Phase just because you prefer that timing. You need a specific rule that changes its Speed.

Run the Invader Phase in order

Resolve each step across the whole island before moving to the next. Before resolving an Invader Card, identify the affected lands and choose their order. A land with no Invaders at that point cannot join that card's Ravages later. Invaders moved into an already-listed land before its Ravage resolves take part in that Ravage. If an action causes a win or loss, finish it and its triggered actions before checking; do not wait for the entire phase.

  1. Blighted Island: carry out any start-of-Invader-Phase instruction on the face-up Blight Card. With no card, or no such instruction, there is nothing to do here. The base game has no Event step.
  2. Fear: reveal earned Fear Cards one at a time, in the order earned. On each, use only the current Terror Level's effect. Cards earned during this Fear step join the end of the same queue.
  3. Ravage: in each land matching the Ravage Card that contains Invaders, deal Invader Damage to the land and to the Dahan, then surviving Dahan counterattack. An empty card space is skipped.
  4. Build: in each land matching the Build Card with at least one Invader, add 1 City if Towns outnumber Cities; otherwise add 1 Town. Do not replace or remove existing pieces.
  5. Explore: reveal the next card. Add 1 Explorer in each matching land that is Coastal, has a Town or City, or is adjacent to a Town or City. Stage III cards affect both listed terrains. The Coastal Lands card affects all Coastal lands.
  6. Advance cards: discard the old Ravage Card, slide Build to Ravage, and slide the newly explored card to Build. The deck waits for next turn's Explore.
Ravage: calculate Damage, Blight, and the counterattack
  1. Add 1 Damage per Explorer, 2 per Town, and 3 per City. Subtract the land's total Defend, to a minimum of 0.
  2. Apply that same remaining amount separately and simultaneously to the land and to its Dahan. Dahan do not absorb Damage for the land.
  3. At 2 or more Damage to the land, add 1 Blight, regardless of how much higher the Damage is. Resolve Presence destruction and any cascade.
  4. Each Dahan has 2 Health. Assign Damage to cause the most deaths possible, accounting for Damage already on each Dahan; apply any leftover Damage to a survivor.
  5. Each surviving Dahan deals 2 Damage to Invaders in that same land—even if wounded or if Defend prevented all incoming Damage. Allocate that counterattack Damage among Invaders as you choose.

Example: A City and an Explorer deal 4 Damage in a land with 1 Dahan. Defend 3 leaves 1 Damage for both the land and Dahan. The land takes too little for Blight; the Dahan is wounded, survives, and still deals 2 counterattack Damage.

Preventing a Build: a matching land builds only if an Invader is still there when Build resolves. Defend changes damage during Ravage; it does not stop a Build.

Resolve Slow Powers in this same turn

Now use the Slow Power Cards you already played and any eligible Slow Innate Powers. Do not play a fresh set of cards and do not pay for the same played card again.

  • Choose legal targets using the island as it looks now. You can affect pieces that just Explored or Built.
  • The card now on Ravage shows the next turn's danger. The card now on Build shows where clearing Invaders can prevent construction next turn.
  • Slow Damage can destroy Invaders wounded earlier this turn; their accumulated Damage remains until Time Passes.
  • Fear Cards earned now wait for the next Invader Phase's Fear step. A newly revealed Terror Level still changes the victory condition immediately.

Slow does not mean next turn. Its timing is later in the current turn. A temporary Defend effect used now expires at Time Passes; it does not normally protect against next turn's Ravage.

Discard played cards and clear temporary effects

  1. Put all your played Power Cards in your personal discard, including cards whose text you chose not to use. Keep unplayed cards in hand.
  2. All surviving Invaders and Dahan heal all Damage. Stand damaged pieces back up.
  3. End this turn's temporary effects such as Defend. Clear reminder markers. Elements from discarded cards are no longer available; uncovered track Elements remain available.
  4. Keep unspent Energy. Leave Presence, Invaders, Dahan, and Blight in place. Keep partial Fear and any earned but unresolved Fear Cards.
  5. If the game has not ended, start another Spirit Phase. Reclaiming cards requires an appropriate Growth option or effect; it is not automatic.

No end-of-round victory delay. Check victory and defeat after an action and its triggered actions finish. Do not complete the rest of the turn after the game ends.

Try the rule checks

Rules reference

Open only the rule needed at the table. These are the base-game defaults; a specific instruction on your components can change them.

The island and your Spirit

Lands, adjacency, Range, and the Ocean
  • A land is one numbered area. Lands are adjacent when they touch, including at a corner; board joins do not block adjacency. Use board letter plus land number to distinguish repeated numbers.
  • Coastal lands touch an Ocean; all other lands are Inland. The Ocean is not a land by default: do not target it, count Range through it, or Push pieces into it without an explicit exception.
  • Range is the shortest chain of adjacent lands from one of your Presence to the target. Range 0 is the starting land; Range 1 includes that land and adjacent lands. Terrain and pieces do not block Range.
  • A Sacred Site requirement changes the starting point to one of your lands with at least 2 of your Presence. Another Spirit's Presence does not help form your Sacred Site.
  • A Spirit-targeting Power usually has no Range limit. "Another Spirit" excludes yourself except in a one-Spirit solo game.
Presence, Energy, and card limits

Presence marks where your Spirit acts. It does not attack, Defend, or block Invaders by itself. Several Spirits may share a land, and a Spirit may place more than 2 Presence there; the land is still only one Sacred Site for that Spirit.

Energy and Elements belong to each Spirit, not the team. You cannot freely give them away. Unspent Energy carries over; unused Card Plays do not. There is no general hand limit, and players may discuss or show their cards.

Destroyed Presence stays off your tracks and off the island unless an effect explicitly returns it. Presence already on the island can be repositioned when adding Presence, but doing so does not improve your tracks.

Powers and movement

Read any Power without memorizing cards
  • Cost: Energy paid when playing the card. Gaining a card does not mean playing it.
  • Elements: symbols supplied while the card is in play, whether or not its text is used. Sun, Moon, Fire, Air, Water, Earth, Plant, and Animal are different Elements.
  • Speed: Fast before the Invader Phase; Slow after it. A Power normally resolves once at its own Speed.
  • Range and target: measure from your own Presence or required Sacred Site, then meet every target restriction. A requirement such as "land with Dahan" does not mean the Power can only affect Dahan.
  • Text and thresholds: follow the text in order. Unless stated otherwise, one Power affects one target, and its temporary effects last only this turn. Changes to pieces stay.
Gain, Forget, Reclaim, and Power Progression

When gaining a Power Card normally, choose the Minor or Major deck, draw 4, keep 1 in hand, and discard the other 3 to that deck's shared discard. Gaining has no printed Energy cost; you pay that cost when you later play the card. If a Power deck runs out, shuffle its matching discard to continue drawing.

After gaining a Major Power, Forget 1 Power Card you own. It may come from your hand, personal discard, or play area—even the card just gained. A forgotten Minor or Major goes to its shared discard; set a forgotten Unique Power aside under your panel. You cannot Forget an Innate Power.

Forgetting a card in play removes its Elements immediately. A temporary effect you already resolved does not normally disappear just because you Forget its card.

Reclaim returns cards from your personal discard to your hand as instructed; it neither gains new cards nor costs a card to Forget. A printed Power Progression replaces the usual draw-4 choice with its next listed card whenever you gain a Power; a gained Major still requires Forgetting.

Gather, Push, and movement without Damage

Gather brings the specified pieces into the target land from adjacent lands. Push sends the specified pieces from the target land into adjacent lands. You choose which eligible pieces and which adjacent lands, up to the printed total. A piece cannot travel several lands within one ordinary Gather or Push.

The Power's Range is checked for the target land, not independently for every origin or destination. You do not need Presence in each of those adjacent lands. Neither movement nor removal automatically deals Damage or generates destruction Fear.

Repeat is an explicit permission, not a second card play

You cannot pay a card's Energy cost again just to reuse it. An effect must permit Repeat. Follow that effect, including the repeat's Energy cost; choose a legal target again and use the Power at its Speed. Repeating does not grant the card's Elements a second time or use an extra Card Play.

Invaders, Damage, and Blight

Destroy a piece when its accumulated Damage reaches its Health. Damage on surviving Invaders and Dahan lasts until Time Passes and does not reduce their attack Damage. Damage to a land does not accumulate across separate actions.

When you deal Damage to Invaders, divide it among Invaders in the affected land as you choose, unless the effect specifies a different allocation. You do not have to destroy Explorers first. You cannot spread one land-targeting Power's Damage across several lands.

Destroying a Town produces 1 Fear; destroying a City produces 2 Fear, in addition to any Fear written on the effect. Remove, Replace, Gather, and Push do not count as Destroy unless a rule says so. Destroying Dahan never gives ordinary destruction Fear.

A Power that says only "Damage" damages Invaders, not Dahan or the land. Dahan are affected only when the effect specifies them. They do not attack or move simply because they share a land with Invaders.

Defend is not "skip Ravage"

Defend reduces Invader Damage to the land and the Dahan for this turn. Multiple Defend effects in the same land add together. Preventing all incoming Damage does not stop surviving Dahan from counterattacking.

If the entire Ravage action is skipped, there is no Dahan counterattack from that action. A land with no Invaders also has no Ravage to counterattack. Dahan do not provide Defend just by being present.

Adding Blight, destroying Presence, and cascading
  1. Take the added Blight from the active supply and place it in the affected land. When adding Blight during play, destroy 1 Presence of each Spirit with Presence there—not every disk.
  2. If the land already had any Blight before this addition, it cascades: add one more Blight to one adjacent land of your choice. The new Blight in the original land stays there.
  3. Apply the same addition rules in that adjacent land. If it was already Blighted, continue the cascade. Each addition can destroy Presence. This is not one extra Blight per existing token and not one in every neighbor.
  4. If the Healthy Island card empties during this process, flip it and follow its instructions before continuing. Add no extra +1 on the new side.

Example: Lands A and B already contain Blight. Adding one to A sends one to B; because B was Blighted too, one more goes to neighboring land C. If C was clean, the chain stops there: 3 new Blight, one in each land.

Old Blight does not continuously destroy Presence added later, and Blight does not automatically damage Dahan or Invaders. When an effect removes Blight, return it to the active supply. This does not turn a Blighted Island back over or restore destroyed Presence.

Build and Explore: the conditions are different

Build needs at least one Invader in a land of the shown terrain. That can be a Town or City with no Explorer. More Towns than Cities means add a City; every other count, including a tie, means add a Town. Add only one building; keep existing pieces.

Explore requires a source: the Ocean for Coastal lands, or a Town/City in the land or an adjacent land. An Explorer alone is never a source. Multiple sources still add only one Explorer to each matching land. Presence, Dahan, and Blight do not block it.

Each card advances from Explore to Build to Ravage over successive turns. Stage III lists two terrain types, but a matching land is not acted on twice merely because the card lists two types.

Fear, victory, and solo play

Fear timing and changing Terror Levels

Move one marker from the Fear Pool to Generated Fear per Fear generated. Whenever the pool empties, earn the top Fear Card face down, restore the pool, and continue with any excess. For this setup the pool contains 8 markers. Earning cards never increases that pool.

A revealed Terror Level divider changes the victory condition immediately. At the Fear step, resolve earned cards in order, using only the current Level on each. Fear Cards earned during that step resolve afterward in the same step; ones earned later, during Ravage or Slow Powers, wait until next turn.

Fear Cards are not your Spirit's Powers. Follow their own instructions; do not impose your Presence or Power Range restrictions on them.

When do we win or lose?
  • Terror I: no Invaders anywhere. Terror II: no Towns or Cities. Terror III: no Cities. Earning the entire Fear Deck also gives victory; the final Fear Card's text need not resolve.
  • Presence loss: the team loses if any Spirit has no Presence left on the island. Disks on tracks do not count.
  • Blight loss: without a Blight Card, an exhausted active supply is a loss. With a card, follow its loss instructions. Emptying the Healthy Island side normally flips it rather than ending the game.
  • Time runs out: the team loses when an Explore requires revealing a card but the Invader Deck is empty—not simply when you reveal its last card. Do not reshuffle it.

Current timing clarification: finish the whole current action and any actions it triggers, then check. If both a win and a loss condition are met, it is a sacrifice victory. A Ravage in one land is one action; you need not finish Ravages in other lands first.

Solo and the limits of this guide

For one Spirit on one board, select 1 player. Use the same turn order, victory conditions, and corrected Blight setup. A Power targeting "another Spirit" may target yourself, but you do not get an extra benefit explicitly conditional on targeting another Spirit.

This tool assumes one Spirit per player and only base-game components. Individual Spirit exceptions remain on your panel and cards. Adversaries, Scenarios, thematic boards, Events, and expansion tokens require their own additional rules; enabling the Blight Card option does not enable those modules.

Frequently missed rules
  • Play and pay for Slow cards in the Spirit Phase, not after the Invaders.
  • Elements are available before a card's text resolves and are never spent on thresholds. You may choose an "Any" Element from a Presence track later in your turn, but cannot change it again that turn.
  • Dahan do not shield the land. Defend protects both separately.
  • A Ravage adds one Blight to its land at 2 or more Damage—not one per 2 Damage.
  • A cascade adds Blight to one adjacent land, not all adjacent lands.
  • Build adds a building; it does not upgrade and remove an existing piece.
  • Card discard and Reclaim are different steps. Cards do not automatically return to hand.
  • There is no limit to the number of Invaders. If figures run out, mark multiple Invaders with Energy tokens.
