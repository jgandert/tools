Randomized Board Generator

The generator tries a limited number of random layouts and keeps the best it finds. It favors different neighboring resources and number tokens and tries to keep 6s and 8s apart. Matching neighbors, including adjacent 6s and 8s, can remain, and results vary each time. This custom layout does not follow the rulebook's prescribed number-disc sequence for variable setup.

Optional Dice-Deck Yield Tool

This optional house-rule alternative to rolling two dice is not part of standard CATAN rules. Its shuffled 36-card set gives each total as many cards as there are two-dice combinations that produce it. With Anti-Card Counting off, a full cycle keeps those counts. When it is on, one random card is removed unseen, leaving 35 draws and one fewer card of that value; the table's remaining column ignores this hidden card. Bandit Grace Period moves 7s out of the first N draws but keeps them in the cycle. Changing either setting reshuffles the deck and clears draw history.

Optional House Rules

These are house rules, not standard CATAN rules, and the tool does not apply them automatically. Agree on any rules you use before play.

The "Poverty is no Shame" Welfare Token
After any player's non-7 production, each player who received no resources from their settlements or cities takes 1 Welfare Token. On your turn, exchange 2 Welfare Tokens with the bank for 1 resource of your choice.
The Friendly Bandit (Edge Case Protection)
When a 7 activates the robber, move it to a different hex that is not adjacent to a settlement owned by a player still at the starting total of 2 victory points. Since Victory Point cards are hidden, agree before play whether they count toward that threshold.
Auction-Based Initial Placement
For variable setup, replace the normal forward-then-reverse placement order with an open auction. Give each player a starting budget of 15 counters. The first player to place names an intersection and opens a bid; others may outbid. The high bidder places their settlement and spends the amount bid. Repeat until all starting settlements are placed. Agree before play what remaining counters break ties for.
Extra Brick and Wood at Setup
Give each player 1 Brick and 1 Wood at setup, in addition to the resources from their second settlement. In standard setup, players receive only the resources adjacent to that settlement.
