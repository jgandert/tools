Set up the table

Use the colored-star side of the player boards. Choose 2–4 players in Guide options; the quantities below adjust automatically.

Step 1 of 53 players · Colored stars

Arrange the shared table

  • Put the scoring board where everyone can reach it.
  • Arrange 7 Factory displays in a circle. Leave the center of the table clear.
  • Place the tower nearby for discarded tiles. Keep the point tokens beside the scoring board.
  • Return unused Factory displays to the box.

The center of the table and the supply spaces on the scoring board are different places. You draft from the first, not the second.

Prepare player boards and scores

  • Give each player one player board, colored-star side up.
  • Take the matching scoring marker and put it on 5 on the scoring track.
  • Leave all star spaces and the four corner storage spaces empty.
  • Leave room beside each player board for collected tiles. There are no starting tiles in your personal area.
  • Return unused player boards and scoring markers to the box.

Fill the bag, supply, and displays

  • Put all 132 tiles in the bag and mix them.
  • Draw 10 random tiles and put them on the supply spaces in the middle of the scoring board.
  • Draw 4 random tiles for each Factory display: 28 tiles across 7 displays.
  • Keep the remaining tiles in the bag. Do not deal tiles to players.

The 10 supply tiles are not available for ordinary drafting. You earn them by completing the spaces around features on your player board.

Set round 1 and its wild color

  • Place the round counter on round 1.
  • Purple is the wild color in round 1. The round track determines the wild color; players do not choose it.
  • Play exactly six rounds, with a different wild color in each one.

A tile keeps its physical color. Being wild only changes how it is collected and how it can help pay for placement this round.

Choose the first player and begin

  • The youngest player takes the first drafting turn. Play continues clockwise.
  • Put the start player token in the center of the table, not in front of the youngest player.
  • Begin Phase 1: Acquire tiles. Everyone collects tiles before anyone places them on a star.

The first player to take tiles from the center claims the token. Its holder starts placement in the same round and drafting in the next round.

Table is ready

3 players · 7 Factory displays · 10 bonus tiles on the scoring board.

Collect first, place later. Build connected groups, claim bonus tiles, and score completed stars and numbers after round 6.

Learn the first round

Learn a round

Learn the rhythm, then drafting, payment, scoring, and passing. Examples are independent practice situations, not a live game.

One round, three separate phases

Your goal is the highest score after six rounds and final scoring. Points come from placing tiles and completing end-game goals; unused tiles can cost points.

  1. 1 · Acquire tilesTake turns until all Factory displays AND the center of the table are empty. Collect tiles beside your player board.
  2. 2 · Play tiles and score pointsTake turns placing exactly one tile and paying for it, or pass. Continue until everyone has passed.
  3. 3 · Prepare the next roundAdvance the round counter and refill the displays. After round 6, skip this phase and finish scoring instead.
Round 1 · Wild color: Purple

Both drafting and placement use clockwise turns. Finish each phase for everyone before starting the next. This is not simultaneous play.

Choose one source, then one non-wild color

On each turn, choose one Factory display OR the center of the table. Take all tiles of one color that is not wild this round. If that source contains wild tiles, you must also take exactly one.

From a Factory display
Put your selected tiles beside your player board. Move every remaining tile from that display into the center of the table.
From the center
Put your selected tiles beside your player board. Leave all other tiles in the center.

Only wild tiles at your chosen source? Take just one. From a Factory display, move the rest into the center; from the center, leave the rest there. You cannot take only a wild tile from a source that still has non-wild tiles.

The first center visit costs points

If the start player token is still in the center, take it with your tiles. Lose 1 point per tile taken this turn, including the wild tile, but do not go below 1. The token itself is not an extra tile penalty. Later visits to the center have no such penalty.

Drafting is mandatory while tiles remain. There is no four-tile limit during this phase, and you do not place tiles on stars yet. The token holder will start Phase 2 and the next round.

Try a drafting example

Pay the printed number; place only one tile

The start player token holder begins. On your turn, choose an empty diamond-shaped space and pay its printed number using tiles beside your player board. One paid tile stays on that space; all other paid tiles go into the tower. Then the next player takes a turn.

Outer stars
The tile placed must match the star's printed color. Pick any empty space you can pay for; you need not start at 1 or place beside another tile.
Center star
Choose any tile color not already placed in this star. Every placed tile here must have a different physical color.
Using wild tiles
Pay with the needed color plus any number of current wild tiles, but keep at least one real tile of the color being placed. Wild substitutes go into the tower; they do not occupy a differently colored space.

You may spend wild tiles even when you have enough matching tiles. A wild-color tile may also be placed as its own color on its matching star, or in the center if that color is absent. In that case, pay entirely in that physical color.

Cost is not score. Paying 4 places one tile, not four, and does not automatically score 4 points. Paid discards have no penalty. Other tiles stay beside your player board for later turns.

Try a payment

Assume an empty space that accepts the chosen color. This checks payment only, not whether that color is already in your center star.

When the chosen color is itself wild, count those tiles only once, in the matching-color field.

Score the connected group, then claim bonuses

Immediately gain 1 point for the new tile, plus 1 for every existing tile connected to it through an unbroken chain of neighboring spaces on the same star. Count the group that contains the new tile, including tiles reached in both directions.

Neighboring spaces share a side around the star. Merely meeting at its central point does not join separate groups. An empty gap breaks the chain; tiles on another star never join it. A completed six-tile star scores 6 for its final placement.

Cover every indicated space around the feature on your player board, not the feature itself. When the last required space is covered, take that feature's bonus immediately. Each completed feature pays once; one placement can complete more than one feature.

Choose freely from the supply spaces, including multiple wild tiles. Put bonus tiles beside your player board for later placement turns in this same phase. Resolve your bonuses, then refill empty supply spaces from the bag before the next player's turn.

The scoring track is continuous. After passing 80 points, use a point token to record the completed lap.

Pass for the round; score after round 6

Passing in Phase 2

When you cannot or do not wish to place a tile, pass. You cannot rejoin this round. Choose up to four remaining tiles to store in the four corner spaces, one per corner. Discard all other remaining tiles into the tower and lose 1 point for each. Other players keep taking turns until they also pass.

Four is the total storage limit, not four per color. Stored tiles score nothing and return beside your player board only for the next round. Their wild status follows the new round color.

After rounds 1–5: prepare the next round

  • The token holder advances the round counter and will take the first drafting turn of the new round.
  • Refill each Factory display with 4 random tiles. If the bag runs out, put all tiles from the tower into the bag and continue drawing.
  • Return the start player token to the center of the table. Its previous holder still starts this round.
  • Everyone moves stored corner tiles beside their player board. Tiles already placed on stars stay there.

After round 6: final scoring

There is no seventh round. After everyone passes, skip preparation and add all completed-star and number bonuses below to the points already earned.

Rules reference

Open the rule needed at the table. These are the standard rules for the colored-star side.

Drafting and turn order

Wild colors and the one-wild drafting rule
  1. Round 1Purple
  2. Round 2Green
  3. Round 3Orange
  4. Round 4Yellow
  5. Round 5Blue
  6. Round 6Red

At one chosen source, take every tile of one non-wild color and exactly one wild tile if any are there. If that source contains only wild tiles, take one. Factory leftovers move to the center; center leftovers remain there. The rule is per source, not across the whole table.

The one-wild restriction does not apply to bonus tiles: you may choose several wild tiles from the scoring-board supply.

Start player token and the center penalty
  • Round 1 starts with the youngest player; the token begins in the center.
  • The first player taking tiles from the center takes the token and loses one point per tile taken, including wild tiles, stopping at a total of 1. The token itself adds no penalty.
  • Its holder starts placement this round and drafting next round. Return the token to the center during preparation, without changing who starts.
  • Later center visits have no token penalty. You may not pass during drafting.

Placement, points, and bonuses

What can pay for a placement?

A space numbered N costs N tiles in total. Place one physically matching tile and discard the other N−1 paid tiles into the tower with no point penalty. Current wild tiles can replace any of those discarded tiles, but never the real tile placed in another color.

Choose any empty diamond space you can legally pay for. There is no ascending-number requirement or mandatory adjacency. Never place on a pillar, statue, or window. The center star cannot repeat a physical color.

To place a tile of the wild color as itself, pay entirely with tiles of that color. A center-star space follows the same payment rule, with the additional no-repeat restriction.

Which tiles score together?

Count the newly placed tile and the uninterrupted group it now belongs to on that one star. Follow neighboring spaces in both directions, counting each tile once. Gaps, other stars, and a mere meeting at the star's center do not connect groups. A final placement in a full star scores 6.

The printed number is a tile cost, not a point value. Completed-star and number bonuses are added only in final scoring.

Bonus tiles do not give an extra placement immediately. They become available for your later turns in the same placement phase.

Passing and game end

Passing, four-tile storage, and the last round

Passing is optional when you could place, and required when you cannot. It ends your placement turns for the round. Keep at most four tiles in the corners; discard all other remaining tiles for −1 point each. The stored tiles return beside your board during next-round preparation, with the new round's wild color.

In round 6, finish the placement phase and add final bonuses. Then discard any tiles still stored in the corners for −1 point each. Every unused tile is penalized once, whether discarded when passing or after final bonuses.

Empty bag and refilling displays

When the bag runs out during a refill, move all tiles from the tower into it and continue. Do not clear unused bonus-supply tiles between rounds. After rounds 1–5, refill Factory displays to four tiles each. If both the bag and tower are empty, begin the next round with any displays still short of tiles.

Scope and independence
