Set up the table

Work together to collect Relics and escape. Prepare one table task at a time; player count adjusts hands, starting Injuries, and starting Madness.

Step 1 of 5 4 players · Standard rules

Prepare the route

Put the Mountain Board in the centre. Each player takes one Player Board, with either side up.

  • Sort the Encounter tiles by region. Randomly fill the matching spaces face down : 11 Coast, 7 Mountain, 3 City, 1 Edge of Madness, and 3 Escape.
  • Return all unused Encounter tiles to the box without looking at their fronts.
  • Leave the Airplane beside the board for now. The first Leader chooses its starting Coast space in the first Movement Phase.

Separate the card supplies

Keep cards that can enter the Equipment deck separate from cards that stay beside Player Boards.

  • Shuffle the 40 ordinary Equipment cards into a face-down draw deck. The four types are Crates, Tools, Weapons, and Books; ordinary values are 2–6.
  • Make separate face-up supplies of 8 Arcane Equipment cards, 15 Injury cards, and 11 Relic cards. Do not shuffle these into the starting draw deck.
  • Sort the printed Madness cards into Levels 1, 2, and 3. Shuffle each level separately and leave all three decks face down. Set the blank cards aside.
  • Put the 30-second Sand Timer, Penalty die, and Relic tokens within reach. Leave room for an Equipment discard pile.

Seed the discard and deal hands

The starting Injuries go into the discard pile , not onto the draw deck and not into anyone's hand.

  • Put 4 Injury cards face up in the Equipment discard: one per player.
  • Deal 4 Equipment cards to each of the 4 players.
  • Players may inspect their own hands, but may not show them or discuss their contents now.
  • Keep all remaining Equipment cards as the draw deck. Do not mix the starting Injuries into it yet.

Later, the Draw Phase refills hands to this base limit. It does not give everyone this many additional cards.

Choose the Leader and starting Madness

The first Leader is the player who most recently reached a mountain summit. Put the Sled Board and Airplane in front of that player.

  • Place all 6 Leadership tokens on the Sled Board. The Leadership discard area starts empty.
  • Give one Level 1 Madness card to every player except the first Leader.
  • Read your own Madness privately, then keep it face down beside your Player Board. Never reveal or explain it, even between rounds.
  • Apply Madness during the Encounter while the Sand Timer runs, not throughout every phase. Madness instructions can override the usual communication rules.

Standard rules: upgrading Madness replaces the old card; do not stack its effects.

Give the table its final briefing

You win together by surviving through the third Escape round and finishing with at least as many Relic cards as Injury cards in the expedition. A tie is enough.

  • Discuss hand contents only while the Encounter timer runs and before the first card is played. The first card immediately ends all communication , including an Injury card.
  • Match each required Equipment type separately. Too much can fail just as easily as too little.
  • One successful Challenge is enough for the printed reward, once. Every failed Challenge still needs its own consequence.
  • Immediate defeat: the last Leadership token must go into the box, or you must draw an Injury when its supply is empty.

This guide follows the revised German rules in both languages. Before playing, check the edition note about forbidden speech .

Learn the first round

Learn a round

Follow all five phases in order; only Rest is optional. The current Leader makes the final decisions and pays Leadership costs from the shared Sled.

Move once; the Leader decides

Discuss the route without discussing your hands. The Leader has the final decision and must move the Airplane.

  1. First round: choose a Coast tile in the bottom row.
  2. Later rounds: move to one adjacent space. You may climb, descend, or remain at the same height, but cannot stay on the same space.
  3. If that space has a tile, put the tile face down in front of the Leader. Place the Airplane in the vacated space. Reveal the tile only when the Encounter starts.
  4. If the space is empty, use its two printed Challenges. There is no reward, but failures still apply.

Leadership option: discard 1 token from the Sled to let one chosen player ignore their Madness for the rest of this round. Spend more to help more players. This does not remove Relic restrictions.

30 seconds; the first card means silence

Turn the Sand Timer and reveal the Encounter tile together. Everyone now follows their current Madness, unless it was suppressed during Movement.

  1. Discuss
    Describe hand contents; do not show cards.
  2. First card
    Face down on the Sled. All communication stops.
  3. Keep playing
    Add cards silently in any order while time remains.
  4. Time expires
    No more cards. Stay quiet for the Leader's decision.

Any player may start playing, and may contribute multiple cards or add more later. An Injury contributes no Equipment but can be played to clear space in hand. It also triggers silence if it is the first card.

Leadership option: just after time expires, the Leader alone may discard 1 Sled token to restart the Encounter timer. Discussion opens again until another card is played. Repeat only at a timer's end; earlier Madness suppression lasts through every restart.

Forbidden speech: under the revised German rules, immediately discard 1 Leadership token if someone speaks while communication is forbidden. Madness instructions override normal communication rules. See the edition note for the different penalty in the English transcript.

Check totals, take the reward, then resolve failures

  1. Reveal: turn over the cards on the Sled. Apply any Relic restriction affecting the current Leader before calculating the result.
  2. Compare: total each required Equipment type separately. A single value must be matched exactly; a range includes both ends. Below or above the allowed value means failure.
  3. Reward: if at least one Challenge succeeds and the tile shows a reward, receive that reward once. Take it before dealing with any failures.
  4. Failures: Choose the order of failed Challenges. For each one, the Leader either gives one eligible player more Madness or rolls the Penalty die. Resolve that consequence fully before choosing the next.
  5. Clear the Sled: put the played cards into the Equipment discard, except cards already removed by an effect. Remove the Encounter tile from play.

Players at Level 3 cannot be chosen for more Madness as a failure consequence. If everyone is at Level 3, every failed Challenge requires a Penalty die roll.

Leadership option: before applying a Penalty die result, discard 1 Sled token to reroll. You may pay again to reroll the new result. Check the Leader's Relic restrictions first.

Refill hands; do not automatically reshuffle

With 4 players, refill each hand to 4 cards.

  • Keep unplayed cards. The Leader deals only the cards needed to reach each player's current hand limit. Injuries occupy hand space; Relic and Madness cards do not.
  • If the Equipment deck cannot supply everyone, the Leader chooses who receives the remaining cards. An empty draw deck is not an immediate defeat.
  • Do not refill the deck from the discard during this phase. Reshuffling belongs to the optional Rest Phase, which comes afterward.

Leadership option: each Sled token discarded increases every player's hand limit by 1 until the next Draw Phase. Extra tokens stack. Deal up to the new limit, as far as the deck allows.

One token This Draw Phase: limit 5 for everyone.

If you are still above the base limit when the bonus expires, keep the excess. Do not receive new cards while already at or above the current limit.

Rest is optional, and permanently costs a token

The Leader may choose Rest after the Draw Phase. Check Relic restrictions first. If resting, carry out these steps in order.

  1. Return 1 Leadership token to the box , from the Sled or its discard area. This token is permanently gone.
  2. Recover the remaining discarded Leadership tokens onto the Sled, subject to the current Leader's Relic restrictions.
  3. Shuffle the remaining Equipment draw deck and its discard together. Leave hands unchanged. Injuries and earned Arcane Equipment in the discard enter the new draw deck too.

Rest does not give a second Draw Phase. With only one Leadership token left anywhere in play, Rest is not allowed: its cost would remove the last token.

End of the round

After the third Escape tile, check the final result. Otherwise, pass the Sled Board to the player on the left, whether you rested or not. That player becomes the next Leader.

To win after escaping, collected Relic cards must be at least equal to Injuries in all hands, the Equipment draw deck, and its discard combined. Do not count the unused Injury supply. See Game end .

Rules reference

Open only what the table needs. Use the actual Relic and Madness cards for their individual instructions.

Leadership and timing

Five phases, five uses of Leadership
The first four phase abilities use Sled tokens. Relics may restrict the Leader.
Phase Use
Movement Discard 1 Sled token per chosen player. Ignore that player's Madness for the rest of the round.
Encounter After time expires, discard 1 Sled token to restart the timer and reopen discussion until another card is played.
Resolution Discard 1 Sled token to reroll the Penalty die before applying its result. Repeat by paying again.
Draw Discard 1 Sled token for +1 to everyone's hand limit until the next Draw Phase. Additional tokens add further +1.
Rest Return 1 token from the Sled or discard to the box; recover the remaining discarded tokens and reshuffle Equipment.

Discarding is recoverable; returning a token to the box is permanent. Count tokens on the Sled and in its discard when checking whether the last token would be removed.

What may we say, and when?
  • Hand contents may be discussed only during the timed Encounter discussion, before the first card of that discussion is played. Never show hand cards unless a Madness instruction requires an exception.
  • After that first card, all communication stops, not only talk about cards. You may still play more cards while time remains.
  • A Leadership restart opens another discussion window. It does not end Madness suppression from this round's Movement Phase.
  • Your Madness remains secret even after the timer ends. Outside the Encounter, discuss route and public game state, not private hands or your Madness instructions.

Rewards and Madness

The five reward types
Relic · Specimen, Ruin, Knowledge
The Leader takes the indicated Relic card and gives it to any player, including themselves. Keep it face up, outside the hand. Resolve its immediate Madness instruction. Put the matching Relic token on the area of the Player Board indicated by the card. Restrictions matter when that player is Leader; follow the actual card. A player can hold several Relics.
Search
Reveal any one face-down Encounter tile for 30 seconds, then turn it back face down in the same place. Adjacency is not required, and the Airplane does not move. Leadership tokens cannot extend this peek.
Arcane Equipment
Choose one available Arcane Equipment card and put it in the Equipment discard. Its value is 10. Do not put it directly in a hand or on top of the draw deck.
Heal
Return one Injury from the Equipment discard to the Injury supply. You may instead heal an Injury just played on the Sled this round. You cannot take one from a hand or search the draw deck for one.
Praise
Move up to two discarded Leadership tokens back onto the Sled, unless a Relic restriction prevents recovery. Tokens in the box are never recovered.

At least one successful Challenge earns the printed reward once, not once per success. No reward is available on an empty board space, or if the tile has no reward.

Additional Knowledge cards do not add another Knowledge token to the same player. Keep every Relic card for the final count.

Gaining Madness and the Level 3 limit
  1. No Madness yet: draw one Level 1 card.
  2. At Level 1 or 2, return the old card to the box and draw one card of the next level.
  3. Already at Level 3: the Leader cannot choose that player to gain Madness for a failed Challenge. If all players are at Level 3, roll the Penalty die for failures.

If Madness is nevertheless required when everyone is at Level 3, discard 1 Leadership token from the Sled instead. If the Sled is empty, return 1 discarded token to the box. Losing the last token ends the game immediately.

For a Relic received at Level 3, follow the card's immediate instruction to discard a Leadership token instead of gaining another Madness card. Do not invent a Level 4.

Do not teach or reveal individual Madness cards to the group. Each owner reads their own instruction privately and follows it during timed Encounters.

Optional Expert variant

In the Expert variant, keep earlier Madness cards when moving to a higher level. All retained effects apply together; the higher-level instruction wins a conflict. The maximum level is still 3. The standard game replaces the old card instead.

The Expert variant switch in Guide options changes the upgrade reminders throughout this guide. Leave it off for a first rules explanation.

Failures and card flow

All three Penalty die results

For a failed Challenge, choose Madness or a die roll, then finish that consequence before the next failure. If you roll, you may pay for a permitted reroll before applying the result.

Accident
Add 2 Injury cards from the supply to the Equipment discard. If you cannot supply an Injury that must be drawn, lose immediately. Taking the last available Injury is not, by itself, a defeat.
Confusion
For that failed Challenge, find the gap between the matching Equipment total and the required value. For a range, use the nearer end: its lower end when short, its upper end when over. Discard that many cards from the Equipment draw deck. Under the revised German rule, if there are not enough cards, return 1 Leadership token to the box instead. Do not automatically reshuffle.
Loss of Confidence
With at least 2 Leadership tokens on the Sled, discard 2. With fewer than 2 on the Sled, return 1 token to the box instead. Removing the final token is an immediate loss.

Practice example: a 9–11 requirement with a played total of 14 has a gap of 3. Confusion would discard 3 cards, not 14 and not 5.

Where do cards go?
Played Equipment and Injuries
Usually move from the Sled to the Equipment discard after Resolution. Playing an Injury does not heal it.
Earned Arcane Equipment and new Injuries
Enter the Equipment discard. They can reach hands after that discard is shuffled back during Rest.
Healed Injuries
Return to the Injury supply; they no longer count against the expedition unless drawn into it again.
Relics and Madness
Stay outside the hand and Equipment deck. Relics are face up; Madness is face down. In the standard game, replaced Madness goes to the box.

The Equipment deck is reshuffled only by Rest, not automatically on depletion. Draw comes before Rest, and Rest does not refill hands a second time.

Escape and final checks

Game end and the equality rule
Immediate loss · final Leadership token removed
Lose when the last token still in play must go to the box. A token in the discard remains in play; an empty Sled alone does not end the expedition.
Immediate loss · an Injury cannot be drawn
Lose when a required Injury draw cannot be completed because its supply is empty. An empty Injury supply without a required draw does not end the game.
Escape · finish the third Escape round
Reaching the third Escape tile does not skip its Encounter or failures. After finishing the round, compare all collected Relic cards with all Injuries in the Equipment deck, its discard, and every hand. Do not count unused Injuries in their supply.

Relics ≥ Injuries: everyone wins. Fewer Relics: everyone loses. Count Relic cards, not reminder tokens; a tie is a win.

Frequently missed rules
  • Starting Injuries are in the Equipment discard, not the draw deck.
  • The first played card ends communication; you do not get to keep talking until the timer ends.
  • Too much Equipment can fail. One success gives one reward, but every failure still hurts.
  • Healing can remove an Injury just played this round. Simply playing it is not healing.
  • In standard play, a new Madness level replaces the old card; it does not add a second effect.
  • Rest permanently costs one token. Discarded tokens and tokens in the box are different.
  • Pass the Sled left after every non-final round, not only after Rest.
  • A tie between collected Relics and expedition Injuries is a win after escape.

Edition note and scope

Rules basis: this guide follows the revised German booklet in both languages. Switching languages changes the presentation, not the rules. Follow the booklet in your copy if it differs.

Forbidden speech: A player who speaks when communication is forbidden discards 1 Leadership token. The revised German booklet and the publisher’s English rules agree on this penalty.

If Confusion requires more Equipment cards than the deck contains, return 1 Leadership token to the box instead. The revised German booklet and the publisher’s English rules agree on this fallback.
