Set up the table

Prepare the shared table, then each Forest. Choose 2, 3 or 4 players in Guide options to adjust the tree supply. This guide covers the base game only.

Step 1 of 5 3 players

Prepare the shared boards

Keep the centre and all shared supplies within everyone's reach.

  • Place the Circle of Spirits in the middle. Put the Guardian Animal board and Fire Varan board beside it.
  • Sort the Fire tiles into values 2, 3 and 4 on the Guardian Animal board. Place one value-2 Fire tile in the Circle of Spirits.
  • Place all 23 Fire Varan cards and 20 Fragment tiles on their supply spaces. Nobody receives a Fragment tile at setup.

Reveal the Guardian Animal reserve

Separate the starting cards from the three shared levels before shuffling.

  • Make three face-down decks: 23 Level 1 cards with a white sun, 16 Level 2 with a yellow sun, and 12 Level 3 with a red sun. Shuffle each deck separately.
  • Reveal four cards per level beside its deck: 12 face-up cards altogether, regardless of player count.
  • Keep the 14-card starting sets separate for the players. Fire Varans do not enter the reserve.

Adjust the Protective Tree supply

Place the 39 non-starting Protective Trees in the two dispensers, ordered by cost. Match both cost and effect to the dispenser slot.

    Costs 3, 4 and 5 each have two different tree types. Apply removals to every type, not just once per price. Starting Trees are never part of these removals.

    Prepare each Forest

    Each player takes one matching set: a Forest board, Spirit of Nature standee, starting Protective Tree, 14 starting Guardian Animals, and three Victory tiles.

    • Put the starting Protective Tree on the centre space of your Forest. It counts as its own tree type but supplies no elements.
    • Place the Fire, Protective Tree and Sacred Flower Victory tiles to the left of your Forest. Each adds 1 to its victory category while you own it.
    • Shuffle your 14 starting cards and place them face down to the right as your personal draw stack. Leave room for a personal discard stack and a face-up Help Line.
    • Return unused player sets to the box. Do not deal a starting hand or reveal cards yet.

    Place the Spirits of Nature

    • Choose the first player at random and give them the Sacred Tree standee. This marks turn order; it is not another Protective Tree in their Forest.
    • On the Circle of Spirits, use the printed starting-position symbols for your player count. The first player uses the rock whose white figure is first in that group.
    • Continue in clockwise seating order: the next player uses the marking with the white figure second, then third and fourth when needed.
    • Begin with Phase A: Guardian Animals. Everyone reveals their own cards; there is no shared starting hand.

    Table is ready

    3 players · 33 shared Protective Trees · 12 face-up Guardian Animals.

    Start with the three ways to win, then reveal some cards and learn how the elements power your actions.

    Learn the first round

    Learn a round

    These five lessons explain a round with three phases. Open one lesson at a time, then use the reference during play.

    Reach one of three targets

    You are competing, not sharing a team score. At the victory check, reach at least 12 in any one category; you never need all three.

    12 different Protective TreesCount different planted tree types, including your starting Tree, plus every Tree Victory tile you currently own. Duplicate planted types count once.
    12 FiresCount collected Fire tiles, not their printed values, plus every Fire Victory tile you currently own.
    12 Sacred FlowersCount flower elements in this round's Help Line, permanent flowers in your Forest and your Flower Victory tiles. Flowers are not saved between rounds.

    Finish everyone's actions first. Check victory before fire attacks or discarding Help Lines. A Victory tile can change hands before the check, so reaching 12 during your own turn is not an immediate win.

    Three phases, in order

    A · Guardian AnimalsSimultaneously build your own Help Line, stopping voluntarily or at three uncancelled solitary symbols.
    B · ActionStarting with the Sacred Tree, each player completes all their regular and bonus actions, then the next seated player acts clockwise.
    C · End of Game turnCheck victory. If nobody qualifies, defend against Fires, add Fires, refill the reserve, discard Help Lines and pass the Sacred Tree.

    Reveal one card, then decide

    Reveal the top card of your personal draw stack. Add it to your Help Line with the left-hand symbols visible. After each reveal, either continue or stop. You do not play cards from a hand.

    • A solitary symbol is the dark circle; a gregarious symbol is the light circle. Each gregarious symbol cancels one solitary symbol. Neutral cards have neither.
    • At three uncancelled solitary symbols, stop revealing. You cannot draw another card hoping it will be gregarious. The last card stays in the Help Line and its elements still count.
    • Finish below three: two different regular actions. Finish at three: one regular action. Cancelling a solitary symbol never removes that animal's elements.
    • Immediately after revealing a card, you may return one Fragment tile to the supply to remove that just-revealed card. A Fire Varan returns to the supply; any other Guardian Animal goes to your discard stack. Recheck the symbols, then continue or stop.

    Only when you need to draw from an empty personal draw stack, shuffle your personal discard to make a new draw stack. Do not shuffle the current Help Line. If both stacks are empty, there is no card left to reveal.

    Try the reveal / stop decision

    A scripted symbol exercise, not a real deck. You start with two Fragment tiles earned in an earlier round. In the real setup you start with none. Reveal, decide, and watch the uncancelled solitary total.

      Turn elements into actions

      Complete your turn before the next player acts. With two regular actions, choose different types unless you own the cost-11 Protective Tree. Resolve each action and any triggered bonus actions fully.

      For each action, total its matching elements in your Help Line and Forest, including completed-line bonuses and any printed negative values. Elements are not spent. Recalculate when a new tree or bonus changes your total.

      Take a Fragment tile

      Take one available Fragment tile. No elements are required. It can help in a later Guardian Animals phase, not retroactively in the phase that just ended.

      Attract one or more Guardian Animals

      Use the sun elements. Choose one or more face-up reserve cards whose combined costs fit your total. Put them face down on top of your personal draw stack, in your chosen order. Do not refill the reserve yet.

      Example: with 8 suns, cards costing 3 and 5 fit together. They will be revealed before the cards that were already in your draw stack.

      Extinguish the Fire

      Use the water-drop elements. Take one or more Fire tiles from the centre whose combined values do not exceed your total. Keep them beside your Forest, value side down. Each tile counts as one Fire towards victory.

      Example: with 6 water, two value-3 Fires earn two tiles. A single value-4 Fire earns only one. You cannot take a Fire from the supply or from another player.

      Move forward on the Circle of Spirits

      Use the spiral elements. Move clockwise up to that many unoccupied rocks. Jump occupied rocks without counting them; take one Victory tile per jump if available. After moving at least one rock, perform the arrival rock's bonus action.

      Plant one and only one Protective Tree

      Use the sprout elements. Take exactly one affordable Protective Tree, not several with a shared budget. Plant it on a free space of your Forest, touching an existing tree along an edge. Its effect applies immediately.

      Sacred Flowers are not an action. They contribute to a victory condition. Your Victory tiles also contribute only to victory totals; a Tree Victory tile is not a tree space and a Flower Victory tile is not an action resource.

      Use immediate and lasting bonuses

      Trees improve your future rounds. Movement and corner spaces give extra actions now. Neither kind of bonus replaces a regular action.

      Build your Forest

      • A new tree must share an edge with an existing tree. Diagonal contact alone is not enough. Never cover another tree.
      • Every planted tree's printed effect is immediately available, including for bonus actions and your second regular action. The starting Tree has no element effect.
      • Identical trees are legal and all their element effects apply, but only one tree of each type counts for the tree victory condition.
      • The cost-11 tree lets you repeat the same regular action when your Help Line allows two regular actions. It does not turn one action into two.

      Travel around the Circle

      • Count only unoccupied rocks and stop on an unoccupied rock. You may move fewer steps than your spiral total. With zero or a negative total, you cannot move.
      • For each Spirit you jump, choose one Victory tile they currently hold, even if it originally belonged to someone else. You do not take collected Fire tiles or planted trees.
      • Jumping the same Spirit twice can take two Victory tiles. If they have none, gain nothing from that jump.
      • Only your arrival rock grants its bonus action. You must actually move; staying on a rock never triggers its bonus again.
      Forest rows, columns and corners

      Complete the three marked central columns for the printed permanent bonuses: 1 water, 1 sun and 1 spiral respectively. Complete the middle row for 2 permanent Sacred Flowers. Merely leaving a printed reward uncovered does not activate it.

      Planting in a corner grants the indicated one-time bonus action: attract with your normal sun total +3, extinguish with your normal water total +2, or take 2 Fragment tiles at either of the two other corners. The +3 and +2 apply only to that bonus action, not to later actions or fire defence.

      A bonus action may repeat a regular action. Use the full relevant element total again; nothing was spent. Example: moving can trigger extinguishing as a bonus, and extinguishing can still be your second regular action.

      Victory check, then upkeep

      First, check victory after the complete Action phase. If anyone meets a target, stop here: do not defend against Fires, add Fires, or discard Help Lines. Otherwise carry out these five steps in order.

      1. Defend against the remaining Fires. Add their values. Each player compares that sum with their own full water total. Equal or higher: no penalty. Lower: add one Fire Varan to your personal discard for each remaining Fire tile, not for each point of fire strength. The Fires stay in the centre.
      2. Add new Fires. For each Guardian Animal attracted this round by anyone, add a Fire: Level 1 → value 2, Level 2 → value 3, Level 3 → value 4. Add value-2 Fires first, then 3, then 4. Stop as soon as the centre holds seven tiles, including old Fires. If the centre is still empty after this step, add one value-2 Fire.
      3. Refill the Guardian Animal reserve. Replace cards taken this round from their matching level decks, where possible. Do not count an old, permanently empty reserve space as a new purchase in later rounds.
      4. Discard the Help Lines. Move each player's face-up line to their personal discard stack. The unspent draw stack stays as it is, with newly attracted animals still on top.
      5. Pass the Sacred Tree clockwise. The next seated player becomes first player. Start the next Guardian Animals phase; do not reset the standees on the Circle of Spirits.
      Try the fire-defence check

      Enter Fires that remain after everyone's actions and your current water total from Help Line + Forest. This checks defence only; it never extinguishes or removes a tile.

      Example values

      The displayed penalty assumes enough Fire Varans remain in the supply. With a shortage, distribute the available cards one at a time in turn order among affected players.

      Newly added Fires do not attack in the same end-of-round sequence. Players get the next Action phase to extinguish them first. Defence uses your full current water total (Help Line + Forest) even if you already extinguished Fires this round.

      Rules reference

      Open the rule needed at the table. Component-specific effects can change a default; this guide does not cover expansions, promos or the separate solo variant.

      Drawing and action timing

      Which elements count, and are they spent?

      Use the visible elements of the current Help Line, all planted tree effects and earned row/column bonuses. Subtract printed negative values. You may use a Forest-only total even when no card shows that element. Elements neither run out through use nor accumulate across rounds. Repeating an action through a bonus uses the full total again.

      Symbol descriptions used here: sun → attract, water drop → extinguish and defend, sprout → plant, spiral → move, flower → victory. A price printed on a card or tree is not an element it supplies.

      Fragments, Fire Varans and reshuffling

      A Fragment can target only the card just revealed in the Guardian Animals phase. It can remove the card that would have caused your third uncancelled solitary symbol. You cannot remove an earlier card, a card in your discard stack or a Fire Varan received as a penalty before it is later revealed. A removed ordinary animal is discarded; a removed Fire Varan goes back to the shared supply. Return the used Fragment too.

      Reshuffle your personal discard only when you need another card and your draw stack is empty. Your current Help Line stays out. You may inspect your discard during the Guardian Animals phase. If precise drawing order matters, use clockwise seating order from the Sacred Tree rather than simultaneous play.

      Regular actions versus bonus actions

      Below three uncancelled solitary symbols you may take two different regular actions; at three, only one. You resolve your whole turn before the next player acts. Bonus actions from arrival rocks or Forest corners are additional, immediate and may match a regular action. The cost-11 tree lifts the different-types restriction, but only when you already have two regular actions. Planting it as your first action makes its effect available immediately.

      Forest and Circle of Spirits

      Different trees, permanent bonuses and corners

      Tree types are not identified by cost alone: costs 3, 4 and 5 each have two types. Duplicates give their effects separately but do not add more different types. The starting Tree and every Tree Victory tile each count as different from all others. Victory tiles do not fill Forest spaces. A planted tree occupies one free space with orthogonal tree adjacency.

      The marked central columns and middle row give permanent elements once filled. Corner bonuses trigger once when a tree is planted there. The corner's +3 suns or +2 water belong only to that immediate bonus action; they are not permanent additions. A new tree's own printed effect is permanent and already usable in its placement bonus.

      Moving, jumping and taking Victory tiles

      Move clockwise, up to your spiral total, counting only unoccupied rocks. Passing an occupied rock takes no movement step. Each jump lets you take one currently held Victory tile from that player; jumping the same player again can take another. Stolen tiles can be stolen again. Collected Fire tiles and planted trees are never taken this way. Receive only the final rock's bonus, and only after moving at least one step.

      Fire, supplies and winning

      Fire strength is not the number of Fires

      For extinguishing and defence, use the sum of printed Fire values. For the penalty, use the number of Fire tiles still in the centre. For victory, count Fire tiles you collected plus your Fire Victory tiles. Example: three value-2 Fires have strength 6, inflict three Fire Varans if your water is below 6, and would be worth three Fires if collected.

      Successful defence prevents the penalty but does not remove Fires. Old Fires remain when new Fires are added. Count actual animals taken this round, not all empty reserve spaces. The centre's limit is seven Fire tiles, not seven fire-strength points.

      What happens when a supply is empty?
      • Fire Varans: if there are too few for all penalties, deal the available cards one at a time to affected players in clockwise turn order, starting from the Sacred Tree. Stop when the supply is empty.
      • Fragments: you cannot gain unavailable tiles. Fragments you use return to the supply.
      • Guardian Animals: refill only as far as the matching deck allows. A purchase still causes its Fire this round; the empty space does not keep creating Fires later.
      • Required value-2 Fire: if the centre needs its single fallback Fire but the value-2 supply is empty, exchange a player's collected value-2 tile for an available value-3 or value-4 tile, then use the freed value-2 tile. The player's Fire count does not change.
      Winning and tied victory conditions

      Make the check after every player has finished the Action phase, while all Help Lines are still present. If exactly one player has at least 12 different Trees, Fires or Sacred Flowers, that player wins. No upkeep follows.

      If several players qualify, only those qualifying players add all three of their victory totals, without capping any at 12. The highest sum wins. A remaining tie is a shared victory. Include currently owned Victory tiles, and use Fire counts, not their printed values.

      The most useful reminders
      • Bought animals go on top of your draw stack, not into the discard. Refill the shared reserve only at round end.
      • The third uncancelled solitary symbol costs you an action, not the whole Help Line.
      • One planting action takes one tree. A bonus may give another planting action, but a large sprout total alone does not.
      • A bonus action may repeat a regular action. Reuse the full elements.
      • Tree victory counts different types; Fire victory counts tiles; flower victory counts the current round.
      • Finish all players' actions, then check victory before upkeep.

      Scope and editions

      Scope: the standard English and German base-game editions for 2–4 players. No character catalogue, expansions, promotional cards or solo rules.

      The English rules do not specify an order for the simultaneous Guardian Animal phase or resolve a remaining tie. This guide uses the German edition's clockwise order from the Sacred Tree and shared victory for a final tie.
