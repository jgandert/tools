Set up the table

Follow one table task at a time. Guide adjusts Corporation Cards, Project Cards, production, and solo rules automatically.

Step 1 of 7 3 players · Beginner Corporations

Prepare Game Board

Place Game Board where every player can read tracks and map.

  • Place 9 Ocean Tiles on Ocean Tile space.
  • Set Oxygen Marker to 0%.
  • Set Temperature Marker to −30°C.
  • Set Generation Marker to 1.
  • Leave Milestones and Awards visible.

Build Resource and Tile Supply

Group shared components beside Game Board.

  • Keep Resource Cubes sorted by value.
  • Place Greenery Tiles and City Tiles within reach.
  • Keep remaining Ocean Tiles together.
  • Keep Special Tiles close to matching Project Cards.
  • Set Player Markers aside for next steps.

Prepare Project Deck

Remove Corporate Era-marked Cards before shuffling.

    Discarded Project Cards remain face down. Reshuffle Discard Pile only if Project Deck runs out.

    Prepare Player Boards

    Give each player one Player Board and one complete color of Player Markers.

    • Place one Player Marker at 20 on Terraform Rating Track.
    • Place one Player Marker on each of six Production Tracks.
    • Set each Production Track to 1.
    • Keep remaining Player Markers near Player Board.

    Seed map with neutral Tiles before preparing your Player Board.

    • Reveal and discard 4 Project Cards.
    • Use each card's cost in order: count City 1 from the map's upper-left across and down, then City 2 backward from the lower-right. Count each Greenery clockwise around its City, starting at upper-left; skip illegal spaces.
    • Place 2 neutral City Tiles.
    • Place one neutral Greenery Tile beside each neutral City Tile.
    • If you choose Tharsis Republic, increase M€ production once for each neutral City, even though they were placed before revealing your Corporation.
    • Do not raise Oxygen Level for neutral Greenery Tiles.

    Assign First Player Marker

    Give First Player Marker to most recent winner.

    • If nobody has played before, agree on a neutral method.
    • Keep player order clockwise.
    • Pass First Player Marker clockwise during Player Order Phase.
    • Do not pass it before generation one.

    Take one Player Board and one complete color of Player Markers.

    • Place one Player Marker at 14 on Terraform Rating Track.
    • Set all six Production Tracks to 0.
    • Keep remaining Player Markers near Player Board.
    • Do not use First Player Marker, Milestones, or Awards.

    Deal Beginner Corporations

    Give each player one Beginner Corporation and 10 Project Cards.

      Beginner Corporations replace normal Corporation choice and opening card purchase.

      Begin Action Phase

      Generation 1 begins differently because setup already handled Player Order and Research.

      • Skip Player Order Phase.
      • Skip Research Phase.
      • First Player takes one or two actions.
      • Continue until every player has passed.
      • Then complete Production Phase.

      Table is ready

      3 players using Beginner Corporations can begin generation 1 in Action Phase.

      First Player takes one or two actions. Play continues clockwise until everyone passes.

      Learn first generation

      Learn a generation

      Open one part at a time. Start with generation rhythm, then learn actions, cards, map rules, and scoring.

      A generation has four phases

      Generation one skips first two phases. Every later generation follows all four in order.

      1. 1 · Player Order Pass First Player Marker clockwise, then advance Generation Marker.
      2. 2 · Research Draw 4 Project Cards. Buy any for 3 M€ each; discard rest face down.
      3. 3 · Action Take one or two actions, then play continues clockwise.
      4. 4 · Production Move Energy to Heat, gain M€, produce resources, and clear used-action markers.
      Passing ends your generation.

      Taking one action keeps you active. Taking no action means you cannot return until next generation.

      Seven available actions

      On your turn, take one or two actions. Same action may be repeated if rules and resources allow it.

      Play a Project Card
      Meet requirements, pay cost, resolve immediate effects, then place Card in correct area.
      Use a Standard Project
      Pay printed price for one of six Standard Projects on Game Board.
      Claim a Milestone
      Meet its threshold and pay 8 M€. Only three Milestones may be claimed.
      Fund an Award
      Reserve an unfunded category for 8, 14, or 20 M€. Only three Awards may be funded.
      Use Blue Card action
      Resolve one action marked by red arrow. Each Blue Card or Corporation action may be used once per generation.
      Convert 8 Plants
      Place Greenery Tile, raise Oxygen Level if possible, and gain corresponding Terraform Rating.
      Convert 8 Heat
      Raise Temperature one step if possible and gain corresponding Terraform Rating.
      Standard Projects and costs
      Project Cost Result
      Sell patents — Discard any Project Cards for 1 M€ each.
      Power plant 11 M€ Increase Energy production one step.
      Asteroid 14 M€ Raise Temperature one step.
      Aquifer 18 M€ Place one Ocean Tile.
      Greenery 23 M€ Place owned Greenery Tile and raise Oxygen Level if possible.
      City 25 M€ Place owned City Tile and increase M€ production one step.

      Read a Project Card

      Buying a Card places it in hand. Playing it later is a separate action with its own printed cost.

      Green · Automated Card
      Resolve once, then overlap it so tags remain visible.
      Blue · Active Card
      Keep face up. Ongoing effects remain available; each marked action may be used once per generation.
      Red · Event Card
      Resolve once, then keep face down in personal Event pile. Tags apply only while Event is played, but Card points still score.
      Paying with metals: Steel pays only for Building-tag Cards at 2 M€ per Resource Cube. Titanium pays only for Space-tag Cards at 3 M€ per Resource Cube. Overpayment is allowed; no change is returned.

      Complete three global parameters

      Raising Oxygen Level, Temperature, or ocean coverage normally raises your Terraform Rating by one. Completed parameters cannot rise and grant no further rating.

      Temperature · −30°C to +8°C

      Advance in 2°C steps. Reaching −24°C and −20°C increases Heat production; reaching 0°C places an Ocean Tile.

      Oxygen Level · 0% to 14%

      Greenery normally raises Oxygen Level. Reaching 8% also raises Temperature one step.

      Ocean Tiles · 0 to 9

      Oceans occupy reserved areas and have no owner. Placing any Tile beside an Ocean Tile gains 2 M€ per adjacent Ocean Tile.

      Place a Tile

      1. Check Ocean-reserved areas, Special-reserved areas, and Card-specific restrictions.
      2. Place Tile. Add Player Marker to owned Greenery, City, or Special Tiles; never mark Ocean Tiles.
      3. Collect printed Placement Bonus and 2 M€ for each adjacent Ocean Tile.
      4. Resolve Oxygen Level or ocean increase and Terraform Rating when applicable.
      Placement: Place Greenery Tile beside one of your Tiles if a legal adjacent area exists. City Tiles cannot touch another City Tile unless a Card overrides normal rule.

      Finish and score

      When Temperature, Oxygen Level, and oceans all reach targets, finish current generation through Production Phase.

      1. Complete Production Phase.
      2. In player order, give each player one final chance to convert Plants into as many Greenery Tiles as possible. Placement Bonuses and triggered effects still resolve; no other actions occur.
      3. Count points in fixed order.
      Terraform Rating
      Final rating is base score.
      Awards
      5 VP for first, 2 VP for second. No second-place points in two-player session. Ties share a place; tied first removes second place.
      Milestones
      5 VP for each claimed Milestone.
      Game Board
      1 VP per owned Greenery Tile, plus 1 VP to each owned City Tile for every adjacent Greenery Tile.
      Project Cards
      Count printed and resource-dependent points across Active, Automated, and Event Cards.

      Tie-break: Most remaining M€ wins.

      Rules reference

      Open only topic needed. Card-specific text overrides general rules.

      Economy and setup

      Resources and production
      M€
      General currency. Production may fall as low as −5. Production Phase income equals M€ production plus Terraform Rating.
      Steel
      Worth 2 M€ when paying for Building-tag Cards.
      Titanium
      Worth 3 M€ when paying for Space-tag Cards.
      Plants
      Spend 8 as an action to place Greenery Tile.
      Energy
      All leftover Energy moves to Heat before new resources are produced.
      Heat
      Spend 8 as an action to raise Temperature.
      Milestones

      Claiming costs 8 M€, requires meeting threshold, and scores 5 VP. Each Milestone has one owner; only three total may be claimed.

      • Terraformer: Terraform Rating at least 35.
      • Mayor: Own at least 3 City Tiles.
      • Gardener: Own at least 3 Greenery Tiles.
      • Builder: Have at least 8 Building tags in play.
      • Planner: Hold at least 16 Cards when claiming.
      Awards

      No threshold is required to fund. First, second, and third funded Awards cost 8, 14, and 20 M€. Funding does not guarantee winning.

      • Landlord: Most owned Tiles.
      • Banker: Highest M€ production.
      • Scientist: Most Science tags in play.
      • Thermalist: Most Heat Resource Cubes.
      • Miner: Most Steel and Titanium Resource Cubes combined.
      Solo setup and deadline

      Solo always uses Corporate Era Cards, starts at Terraform Rating 14 with all production at zero, does not use Milestones or Awards, and must complete all parameters by end of generation 14.

      Before choosing Corporation and Project Cards, reveal and discard 4 Project Cards. Use their costs to place 2 neutral City Tiles and one adjacent Greenery Tile beside each, following the board-reading method. Neutral Greenery does not raise Oxygen Level. Neutral resources and production may be targeted as if belonging to opponent.

      Parameters and placement

      Global bonus steps

      Player who moves Marker onto bonus space receives bonus: 8% Oxygen Level raises Temperature; −24°C and −20°C each increase Heat production; 0°C places an Ocean Tile. Each resulting global increase normally raises Terraform Rating for player resolving it.

      Tile placement and adjacency
      • Ocean Tiles go only on Ocean-reserved areas and have no owner.
      • Greenery Tile must be adjacent to one of your Tiles when possible.
      • City Tile cannot be adjacent to another City Tile unless a Card allows it.
      • Placement Bonus goes to player placing Tile.
      • Gain 2 M€ for each adjacent Ocean Tile when placing any Tile.

      Projects and timing

      Requirements and incomplete effects

      Requirements matter when Project Card is played, not when its later action is used. Required production changes must be possible. A Project Card may still be played if only impossible part would raise a completed global parameter, add unusable resources, remove optional red-bordered resources, or place Tile when no legal area remains.

      Red borders and effect order

      Red-bordered resources and production may target any one player, including you; resource removal can be partial or skipped. Red-bordered production loss is mandatory: if no opponent can lose the required amount, lower your own production or you cannot play the card. Red-bordered tile effects may target any or all players' tiles; a red-bordered tag effect affects all cards with that tag. When effects trigger together, the active player chooses their order.

      Common turn questions
      • You may take one action instead of two and remain active.
      • Once you pass, you are out for rest of generation.
      • Same Standard Project may be used repeatedly if you can pay.
      • Blue Card actions are once per generation; ongoing effects are not actions.
      • Unbought Research Cards are discarded. Bought Cards remain in hand without limit.
      • Placement Bonuses and Ocean Tile adjacency income apply to Tile placed by Card or Standard Project.
